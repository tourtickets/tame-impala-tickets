The Deadbeat Tour

A 360° show built around Kevin Parker's fifth album

The Deadbeat Tour is Tame Impala's first in-the-round production — a stage placed at the center of the arena instead of against one wall, so the crowd surrounds the band on every side. It's currently finishing its North American run before heading to Australia in October 2026, then a full UK, European, and festival leg that carries the tour through July 2027.

Wherever it lands, expect Deadbeat's guitar-forward, woozy songwriting delivered with the most immersive staging Kevin Parker has built for any album cycle to date.

Where It Started

A brief history of Tame Impala

Tame Impala began in Perth, Western Australia, as a home-recording project led almost entirely by Kevin Parker, who wrote, played, and produced nearly every sound himself before ever assembling a touring band. Early releases built a cult following around Parker's ability to make a bedroom studio sound like a full psychedelic rock outfit.

Innerspeaker and Lonerism established that sound on a wider stage, pairing warped guitars with dense, immersive production. Currents marked a turn toward dance and pop songwriting that pulled Tame Impala out of psych-rock circles entirely, and led to Parker's writing and production work spreading across pop and hip-hop. The Slow Rush continued that evolution, and Deadbeat — Parker's fifth studio album — is the record behind this in-the-round Deadbeat Tour.

On stage, the one-man-studio project becomes a six-piece band, translating Parker's layered production into a live show built specifically to fill a room from every angle.

Is Tame Impala selling tickets through Ticketmaster?

Does Ticketmaster sell VIP packages for Tame Impala?

What happens if my show is sold out on Ticketmaster?

Ticketmaster offers official verified resale directly on the artist page for sold-out dates, and platforms like StubHub carry additional resale inventory.

Who is opening for Tame Impala on this tour?

Support changes by region: Dominic Fike is on the late-summer 2026 North American dates after Djo opened the earlier leg, Ninajirachi opens the October 2026 Australian shows, and Wet Leg joins the UK and European arena dates in 2027.

What is Tame Impala's live band lineup?

Kevin Parker leads a six-piece live band: himself on vocals, guitar, and synths, Jay Watson and Dominic Simper on guitars and synths, Cam Avery on bass and synths, Julien Barbagallo on drums, and James Ireland on additional guitar and synths.

Is Tame Impala touring outside North America?

Yes. The Deadbeat Tour heads to Australia in October 2026, then the UK and Europe from late May through mid-July 2027.
